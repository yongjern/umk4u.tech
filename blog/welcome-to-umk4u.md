UMK4U started with a simple question: **which repeated student tasks could be made easier with a focused web tool?** This blog is where we will share what we learn while answering it.

## What you will find here

Expect short, useful posts rather than announcements for their own sake. We plan to publish:

- updates to UMKWifi and MyUMK;
- practical guides for common campus tasks;
- decisions we make while designing and building each product; and
- lessons from maintaining an independent student project.

## Built around real routines

The best ideas usually begin with a small inconvenience that happens often. Connecting a new device to campus Wi-Fi, finding the right academic link, or checking what comes next in the day should not require a long search.

That same principle will guide this blog. Each article should leave you with something clear: a feature you can use, a process you can follow, or a decision you can understand.

## What comes next

We are continuing to improve the two current UMK4U products while listening for the next useful problem to solve. New posts will appear here as those improvements ship.

Thanks for following the project.
